# ExactForm Calculator — Vision and Feature List

**Working title:** ExactForm Calculator (rename freely)
**One-liner:** A real calculator on your phone — TI-style exact math, not Apple's decimal-only toy.

## The idea

Phone calculators (especially Apple's) give you decimal approximations for everything. Real calculators — TI-84, Casio fx series — work in **exact form**: real fraction bars, √2 stays √2, π stays π, and you choose when to convert to decimal. This app brings that full experience to the phone, plus things a physical calculator can't do (deep history, storage, search).

It should *feel* like a proper calculator: a real keypad, textbook-style notation, instant exact answers.

## Who it's for

- Engineering/science students (like Lukas) who need exact form daily
- Anyone who's ever been annoyed that 1/3 × 3 ≠ 1 on their phone
- TI owners who want the same power without carrying a second device

## Core features (MVP)

1. **Exact-form engine** — fractions, surds, π/e symbolic, exact simplification. `√8` → `2√2`, `1/3 + 1/6` → `1/2`.
2. **Form toggle** — one tap switches any result between exact and decimal approximation.
3. **Textbook rendering** — real fraction bars, superscripts, radicals, proper 2D math layout as you type.
4. **TI-style keypad** — familiar scientific layout, haptic feedback, expression history you can scroll and re-edit.
5. **Deep history & storage** — every calculation saved, searchable, recallable into new expressions. Way beyond a physical calculator's memory.
6. **Step-by-step solutions** — show the working (simplification steps), great for learning and checking homework.
7. **Unit conversion + constants** — built-in physical constants and unit conversions (engineering students live on these).

## Later phases

- **Graphing** (Phase 2) — function plotting, trace, intersections. Likely the paid flagship feature.
- **CAS-lite** — symbolic algebra (solve, factor, expand).
- **Matrix & complex number modes**, statistics/regression.
- **Custom functions / programmable keys**, share calculations as images/links.

## What makes it stand out

- Exact form as the *default*, not a buried mode — this is the core differentiator vs Apple/Google calculators.
- Feels like hardware: keypad-first design, not a chat box with a keyboard.
- Steps + history make it a learning tool, not just an answer box.
- Clean, fast, offline-first. No account needed for core use.

## Monetization (draft — see `02 - Research / Monetization Plan`)

- Free: full scientific calculator, exact form, history.
- **Pro (one-time or subscription):** graphing, CAS features, step-by-step for advanced topics, themes/cloud sync.
- Graphing as the paid hook mirrors how TI segments hardware (TI-84 vs TI-30).
