# ExactForm Calculator — Monetization Plan

*Research completed Oct 8, 2026.*

## How calculator apps actually monetize

| App | Model | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Photomath Plus | Subscription (steps + textbook solutions) | $9.99/mo or $69.99/yr |
| Symbolab Pro | Subscription (steps + practice) | $6.99/mo, $24.90/6-mo, $29.99/yr |
| HiPER Calc Pro | One-time unlock (100-digit precision, no ads) | ~$2.50–4 |
| RealCalc Plus | One-time unlock (fractions, landscape, widget) | ~$3.49 |
| PCalc (iOS) | One-time premium | $9.99 |

**The pattern:** *utility calculators monetize via one-time Pro unlocks; solver/tutor apps monetize via subscriptions for step-by-step content.* Calculator users resist subscriptions for a tool, but happily pay once — and pay recurring for learning content.

## Recommended model for ExactForm

### Tier 1 — Free (the acquisition engine)
Full exact-form scientific calculator, offline, **no ads**. This alone beats HiPER's ad-supported free tier and Apple's decimal-only calculator. Free must be genuinely great — it's the marketing.

### Tier 2 — ExactForm Pro, one-time $4.99–9.99 IAP
- Step-by-step solutions
- Calculation history/workspaces with export (PDF/image)
- Themes + customization
- Advanced CAS: limits, integrals, equation systems
- Price point matches PCalc/RealCalc Plus conventions — a no-brainer.

### Tier 3 — Graphing module, paid add-on ($2.99–4.99) or "Pro+" bundle
Exactly as planned: keeps v1 scope tight, creates a second revenue beat at the v2 launch, and graphing is the feature users most expect to pay for (mirrors TI's hardware segmentation: TI-84 vs TI-30).

## Rules

- **No ads, ever.** Premium positioning collapses with a banner.
- **No subscription for the calculator itself.** Reserve subscriptions only if a learning-content product (courses, practice packs) ever ships.
- **Free tier must convert on merit**, not on crippling — crippled free tiers get 1-star reviews; generous free tiers get word-of-mouth.

## Revenue sketch (directional, not a forecast)

At 100K downloads with 3% Pro conversion at $6.99 ≈ $21K gross per IAP wave, before Apple's 15–30% cut and before the graphing add-on. The real lever is volume — the free tier's job is to get there.
